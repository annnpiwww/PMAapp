# PMA App — Production Code, AI Vision & Performance Audit (2026-11-07)

## Summary
- **Overall Verdict**: **6.4 / 10 (Needs Targeted Fixes)** — Core offline-first architecture and recent 2026-11-05 performance fixes are solid, but AI Vision status mapping, negation-blind parsers, and cross-midnight shift/clock-skew guards cause frequent false findings (`temuan` / `MERAH`) and user lockouts in production.
- **Top Risk 1 (False AI Temuan Flood)**: `MaintenanceLogEntry._extractTemuan` (`maintenance_log_service.dart:126-138`), `WhatsAppReportService._hasIssue` (`whatsapp_report_service.dart:319-327,465-472`), and `AiVisionService._parseAiJsonResponse` (`ai_vision_service.dart:996-1046`) turn every `perlu_cek_manual` (timeouts, Mode Kilat, 3/6/12/24 auto-offline backoff, glare) and negated `poinGagal` (`"Tidak ada debu"`) into Map `Temuan`, WA `⚠️` issues, or `MERAH` verdicts, compounded by 420px Q50 downscaling (`image_watermark_processor.dart:87,133`).
- **Top Risk 2 (False Anti-Fraud Clock Skew & Shift Bugs)**: `SecureTimeService.getVerifiedTime` (`secure_time_service.dart:105-134`) compares `DateTime.now()` against `Stopwatch` (`CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, which pauses during Android deep sleep), permanently flagging any technician who locks their phone for >5 minutes as `[JAM DIUBAH MANUAL]`; meanwhile `pulangAllowedFrom` (`absensi_setup_service.dart:199-220`) and `_isRecordLate` (`attendance_archive_screen.dart:322-350`) miscalculate cross-midnight shifts (`22:00-06:00`, `24:00-08:00`).
- **Top Risk 3 (Silent Sync Duplication & Race Conditions)**: `GoogleSheetsService` (`google_sheets_service.dart:156-200,238-256`) treats Google Apps Script HTTP `302` POST redirects as failures (causing infinite retry duplication) and drops 8 payload fields on offline `SocketException`, while `DailyTaskService.claimTask` (`daily_task_service.dart:765-828`) and `_matchesTechnician` (`daily_task_service.dart:209-224`) have overwrite races and 3-letter substring assignment leaks.
- **Part A Trace Hop Chain**: `PointCameraView._takePhotoAndVerify` (`point_camera_view.dart:521,699`) $\rightarrow$ `ImageWatermarkProcessor.generateAiVisionBase64` (`image_watermark_processor.dart:21,87,133`) $\rightarrow$ `AiVisionService.verifyMaintenancePoint` (`ai_vision_service.dart:377,433,793`) $\rightarrow$ `supabase/functions/ai-vision/index.ts:50-119` $\rightarrow$ `_parseAiJsonResponse` / `_parseFreeTextFallback` (`ai_vision_service.dart:965-1202`) $\rightarrow$ `PointStatus` (`point_camera_view.dart:762-789`) $\rightarrow$ `WhatsAppReportService.generateReportText` (`whatsapp_report_service.dart:679,1297`) $\rightarrow$ `MaintenanceLogService.syncLocal` / `_extractTemuan` (`maintenance_log_service.dart:121-141,499-540`) $\rightarrow$ `MaintenanceMapScreen._loadMonthData` / `extractTemuanItems` (`maintenance_map_screen.dart:163-207,457-478,550-565`).

### Direct Answers to Part A Audit Questions (1)–(5)

1. **(A1) Code paths producing `temuan` / `MERAH` on uncertainty, timeout, malformed JSON, or negation**:
   - **Uncertainty / Glare / Blur / Timeout / Offline $\rightarrow$ Map `Temuan` & WA `⚠️`**: `MaintenanceLogEntry._extractTemuan` (`maintenance_log_service.dart:126-138`) includes `PointStatus.perluCekManual` in `temuanList`, and `WhatsAppReportService._isMeaningfulAlasan` (`whatsapp_report_service.dart:319-327`) fails to exclude AI timeout/parse error strings from `ai_vision_service.dart:450,768,1150,1195`.
   - **Negation in `poinGagal` $\rightarrow$ `MERAH`**: `_parseAiJsonResponse` (`ai_vision_service.dart:1030-1046`) maps every string in `poinGagal` through `SopCriteriaHelper.toNegativeStatement` (`lines 118-161`) and then forces `if (poinGagal.isNotEmpty) status = VerificationStatus.tidakSesuai`.
   - **Negation in `rawStatus` $\rightarrow$ `MERAH`**: `rawStatus.contains('tidak')` (`ai_vision_service.dart:997`) runs before `rawStatus.contains('sesuai')` (`line 1002`).
   - **Malformed / Truncated JSON $\rightarrow$ `MERAH` or `perlu_cek_manual`**: `_parseFreeTextFallback` (`ai_vision_service.dart:1089-1199`) checks unnegated `qualityKeywords` (`'blur'`, `'blurry'`, `'tilted'`, `'gelap'`) $\rightarrow$ `VerificationStatus.tidakSesuai`, and falls back to `VerificationStatus.perluCekManual` (`line 1193`) whenever Indonesian text does not contain $\ge 50\%$ of the full `criteria` strings verbatim.
2. **(A2) Prompt contradictions**:
   - **Blur / Dark severity**: Absensi prompts (`ai_vision_service.dart:512,521,547,557`) mandate `blur/gelap = tidak_sesuai (MERAH)`, Maintenance prompt (`line 810,854`) mandates `blur/gelap = perlu_cek_manual (KUNING)`, but shared `_parseFreeTextFallback` (`lines 1089-1120`) forces `blur/gelap = tidak_sesuai (MERAH)` for both.
   - **Rule 10 vs `srv_04`/`kas_02` Thermal Paste**: Rule 10 (`ai_vision_service.dart:900`) forbids requiring visible thermal paste, while `maintenance_checklist_screen.dart:676,834` injects `CRITERIA`: `"Pasta pendingin prosesor baru dioleskan di atas heatsink (tidak kering)"`.
   - **Rule 13 vs `srv_05`/`kas_03` Cable Ties**: Rule 13 (`ai_vision_service.dart:923`) says cables without cable ties must pass if tidy, while `template_repository.dart:452` and `maintenance_checklist_screen.dart:684,760,845` inject `CRITERIA`: `"terikat rapi pakai cable tie/spiral"`.
   - **Rule 9 vs Fallback `bar_${i}_09` Dynabolts**: Rule 9 (`ai_vision_service.dart:891`) forbids checking asphalt dynabolts, while `maintenance_checklist_screen.dart:498` injects `"karet speed bump terpasang dinabolt kuat"`.
3. **(A3) Default/fallback observation text written as a finding when AI failed**:
   - Yes: when AI times out (`ai_vision_service.dart:768`), returns non-JSON (`line 1195`), or hits an unverifiable fallback (`line 1150`), the fallback `alasan` string (`'Koneksi ke AI gagal (timeout). Foto dialihkan untuk Cek Manual oleh Supervisor.'`) is saved on `MaintenancePointResult`, extracted as a `MaintenanceTemuan` in `maintenance_log_service.dart:126-138`, and printed as a `⚠️` finding in `whatsapp_report_service.dart:319-327,465-472,1297`.
4. **(A4) Branch order in `_defaultObservationForTheme` & thematic grouping**:
   - The old barrier `'jalur kabel'` vs Pos Kasir `'stopkontak'` collision at `whatsapp_report_service.dart:1154` is guarded with `!l.contains('stopkontak')`. However, three issues remain: (1) `cleanItemLabel` (`line 235`) converts Manless `'Pembersihan total printer'` into `'Pembersihan printer'`, which hits Booth Parkir's branch at `_defaultObservationForTheme` (`line 1117`: `if (l.contains('pembersihan printer')) return 'printer booth bersih...'`) before Manless (`line 1207`); (2) `_appendBarrierGroupedNotes` Theme 7 (`line 1662`) matches bare `'receiver'`, stealing fallback point `bar_${i}_08` (`'Casing Sensor Receiver Anti Air'`); (3) `_buildBarrierGatePoints` (`maintenance_checklist_screen.dart:480-501`) has shifted point IDs (`bar_${i}_07..09`) compared to `TemplateRepository.defaultTemplates`.
5. **(A5) Temperature / schema / model settings**:
   - `temperature: 0.1` and `response_format: {'type': 'json_object'}` (`ai_vision_service.dart:628,636`) are good, but: (1) `const AiVisionConfig()` constructor sets `maxTokens = 150` (`ai_vision_config.dart:160`) vs `350` in `defaultConfig()` (`line 193`); (2) `attempt == 0` in `_runCustomEndpointVerification` (`lines 672-699`) can run both a 15s proxy timeout and a 15s `callDirect()` timeout back-to-back, exhausting the 30s budget before trying fallback models; (3) `supabase/functions/ai-vision/index.ts:103` forwards `reasoning_effort: 'none'` unchanged when falling back from `gemini-3-flash-preview` to `gemini-3.5-flash`.

## Needs Aan decision

1. **Should `perlu_cek_manual` (glare, blur, AI timeout, or Mode Kilat / Offline photos) count as a Map `Temuan` or only `tidak_sesuai` (`MERAH`)?**
   - Currently, `MaintenanceLogEntry._extractTemuan` (`maintenance_log_service.dart:126-138`) puts both `tidak_sesuai` (`MERAH`) and `perlu_cek_manual` (`KUNING`) into `temuan`, turning the POS orange on the SPV Map and adding it to the "Temuan" tab.
   - **Option A (Recommended)**: Only `PointStatus.tidakSesuai` (`MERAH`) becomes a Map/Sheets `Temuan`; `PointStatus.perluCekManual` is stored separately (or ignored when caused by AI timeout/offline) so the POS stays Green (`Aman`) unless a real defect was detected.
   - **Option B**: Keep `perlu_cek_manual` as a Map `Temuan` when AI judged the photo (e.g. glare/blur), but exclude system/network fallbacks (`Mode Kilat`, `Mode Offline otomatis`, `Koneksi ke AI gagal`).
2. **When a daytime technician checks in late *after* their scheduled shift end (e.g. checks in at `14:05` for `Shift 1 (06.00 - 14.00)`), when should Absen Pulang unlock?**
   - Currently, `pulangAllowedFrom` (`absensi_setup_service.dart:217-219`) sees `shiftEnd < checkInTime` and locks Pulang for `checkInTime + 8 hours` (`22:05`), because the same `shiftEnd.isBefore(checkInTime)` check is used to detect overnight shifts.
   - **Option A (Recommended)**: Detect overnight shifts explicitly from shift start/end hours (`endMinutes <= startMinutes`); for daytime shifts where `checkInTime >= shiftEnd`, allow Pulang immediately (or after a short 15-minute grace window) as promised by the docstring at `absensi_setup_service.dart:243`.
   - **Option B**: Require a minimum work duration (e.g. 4 hours) whenever a technician checks in after their scheduled `shiftEnd`.
3. **For Server & PC Kasir CPU maintenance (`srv_04` / `kas_02`), is dismantling the heatsink to apply fresh thermal paste mandatory on every routine maintenance?**
   - Prompt Rule 10 (`ai_vision_service.dart:900`) explicitly tells Gemini **not** to require visible thermal paste (only a clean fan/heatsink), whereas the checklist `CRITERIA` (`maintenance_checklist_screen.dart:676,834`) tells Gemini to verify `"Pasta pendingin prosesor baru dioleskan di atas heatsink (tidak kering)"`. Confirm whether routine maintenance only requires cleaning the fan/heatsink (update `CRITERIA` to match Rule 10).
