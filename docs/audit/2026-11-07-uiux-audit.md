# PMA App (BssparkingTimeMark) UI/UX & Frontend Feature Audit (2026-11-07)

## Summary
- **Overall Weighted UI/UX Score (`senior-uiux-auditor`)**: **69.2 / 100 — Ship with Fixes** (A. Usability & Flows 13/20, B. Visual Hierarchy & Layout 11/15, C. Interaction & Micro-UX 11/15, D. Accessibility WCAG AA 7/15, E. Mobile Ergonomics & Touch 7/10, F. State Completeness 7/10, G. Copy & Microcopy 4/5, H. Performance & Perceived Speed 3.2/5, I. Design System Cohesion 6/5-scaled).
- **Scope & Visual Proof**: Audited all 6 priority user-facing scopes across Android 360x800 (`textScaler: 1.0` & `1.3`, Light & Dark, with `BackButton` present) and iPhone Web PWA code paths; backed by 6 new 360dp `textScaler: 1.3` golden screenshots in `docs/screenshots/audit-2026-11-07/` plus 12 existing screenshots in `docs/screenshots/`.
- **Top Risk 1 (P0 Data Loss + Web Crash in History Archive)**: Clearing attendance history in `AttendanceArchiveScreen` claims to delete only the active branch's records (`$count catatan`) but wipes all branches via `StorageService.clearAllAttendanceRecords()`, while CSV export crashes on iPhone Web PWA due to unguarded `dart:io` `Directory.systemTemp`.
- **Top Risk 2 (P1 Broken Field Flows in Daily Tasks & Absensi)**: iPhone Web PWA draft recovery in `CustomTaskExecutionScreen` throws on `File(p).existsSync()` and loses typed notes; video-only task completions are invisible in `CompletedDailyTaskModal`; HO Makassar shift cards in `AbsensiKategoriDialog` mismatch `BranchService` (`RSWS`/`JP` duplicates); and filling Pulang handover in `AbsensiKategoriDialog` still triggers a duplicate `DailyPulangBottomSheet` on shutter tap.
- **Top Risk 3 (P1/P2 Contrast & 360dp `textScaler: 1.3` Overflows)**: `GalleryScreen` Light Mode AppBar actions are 1.00:1 invisible (`#0A6B6B` on `#0A6B6B`) and crush the title to `"Fol..."`; `MaintenanceHistoryScreen` Dark Mode empty state is 1.14:1 invisible (`#18181B` on `#0B1120`); and horizontal `RenderFlex` overflows hit `CustomTaskExecutionScreen`, `AttendanceArchiveScreen`, and `GalleryScreen` at 360dp `textScaler: 1.3`.
